What the app uses

Clock Art displays the time, animations and a system screensaver. Your choice of design, colours, animations, display settings, night mode and named profiles is stored locally in the app’s storage on your device. Settings and profiles are not sent to any server operated by the publisher.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on on your device, the system may include the clock settings, saved profiles, favourite designs and language choice in the backup of your Google Account, or transfer them when you move to a new device. The backup is operated by Google according to your device settings, and the publisher has no access to it. The Premium purchase receipt is not included in the backup; Premium is restored on a new device through Google Play. You can turn off backup in Android’s system settings.

The app does not require its own registration or access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone. It contains no ads, no analytics of its own and no own service for automatically sending crash reports.

Premium purchase

The one-time Premium purchase is handled by Google Play through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment according to its own terms and policies. Clock Art has no access to your payment card number.

The app receives the data needed to process and verify the purchase, such as the product identifier, purchase state, token and signed receipt. It stores the verified signed receipt locally so that Premium can be used offline. When verifying, acknowledging and restoring a purchase, it communicates with the Google Play service. The current version does not send the purchase token to any server operated by the publisher.

Google Play services may process further data under their own rules. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy. Restoring a purchase uses the relevant Google Play account. Settings and profiles are restored only through the Android backup described above, not through the purchase.

Retention and deletion

Local settings, profiles and the purchase receipt remain in the app’s storage until you remove them or clear the app’s data. You can delete individual profiles in profile management. Restoring the default settings keeps saved profiles and verified Premium. You can delete all local data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. You manage an existing Android backup in the backup settings of your device or Google Account.

Deleting local data does not delete the transaction record in Google Play and is not a refund of the purchase. Transactions are managed by Google. The app has no user account of its own that would need to be closed.

Support and this website

If you contact us, we will use the information you send us to handle your request. Opening this page uses your web browser and the site’s hosting provider. The page itself contains no analytics script or advertising cookies; the hosting provider may process standard technical access logs under its own terms.

Changes

If the processing of data or the app’s features change, we will update this policy and state the date of the update. For questions or requests, please use the contact above.
